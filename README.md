# Project Oval

**🎮 Play now: https://shadowai-games.github.io/PROJECT-OVAL-FREE-DEMO/**

A physics-based arena shooter where your gun is how you move. Fire at the ground and the recoil launches you into the air — every weapon doubles as a jetpack. Grab ledges, walls, or other players to pull yourself around, and drop blocks, ramps, and walls on the fly to build cover mid-fight.

## What's in this repo

Just the free playable demo — a browser build (playable instantly, works on desktop, phone, and tablet) and a downloadable Windows build. No sign-up, no password, just a name saved locally on your own device.

## Highlights

- **6 playable Eggs**, each with a real, distinct ability — from a flaming shotgun to a shield that halves incoming damage
- **5 game modes** — Team Battle, Deathmatch, Egg Hunt, King Oval, and the timed Oval Rush event
- Real on-screen touch controls for mobile, including full ability use
- A local Battle Pass, Missions, and Ranked stats — no account required

This is a v0.1 playable prototype and still actively growing — new content lands regularly.
